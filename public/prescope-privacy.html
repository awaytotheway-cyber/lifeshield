5. Genetic and health data — special protections

Genetic data (BRCA1/2 test results, SNP panel results) and detailed health data are treated as our most sensitive category. They receive additional protections beyond those described elsewhere in this policy.

Separate, explicit consent

We obtain a separate, specific, informed consent for each category of genetic and specialised testing: BRCA1/2 genetic testing, circulating tumour cell (CTC) testing, and SNP risk-modification panel. You may decline any or all of these without affecting your access to the rest of the Service. Each consent decision is documented with a timestamp.

Access restrictions

Genetic results are accessible only to you and the specific clinician assigned to your care. No other staff member, administrator, or third party can access them without your explicit written authorisation. Genetic data is never included in anonymised analytics or research datasets without a separate research consent that you voluntarily opt into.

No secondary use

We will never use your genetic data for insurance profiling, employment screening, risk scoring for third parties, or any purpose beyond the clinical service you enrolled for. We will never share genetic data with insurance companies, employers, or government agencies except as required by applicable law.

Withdrawal and deletion

You may withdraw consent for genetic data processing at any time by emailing prescopehealth@gmail.com. Upon withdrawal, we will delete your genetic data within 30 days, subject to any retention required by applicable medical records law. Withdrawing genetic consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

6. How we store and protect your data

Security measures

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Database access uses Row Level Security — each user can only read and write their own records; this is enforced at the database level, not just the application level
  • Server-side API keys and secrets are stored as encrypted environment variables, never in app code or client-side bundles
  • Authentication sessions are stored in encrypted on-device storage
  • Clinician access to results requires authenticated sign-in and is restricted by role-based access controls
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our infrastructure and dependencies

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and related laws, you have the following rights. You can exercise any of them by contacting us at prescopehealth@gmail.com.

Right to access

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. We will provide it in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format within 30 days.

Right to correct

You can update or correct your profile and questionnaire responses at any time within the app. For corrections to clinical or genetic data, contact us and we will coordinate with the relevant clinician.

Right to erasure

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data. We will delete non-medically-retained data within 30 days. Health and clinical records we are legally required to retain (see retention table above) will be deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

Right to withdraw consent

Where processing is based on your consent (especially genetic data and specialised testing), you can withdraw that consent at any time. This will not affect processing that has already occurred.

Right to nominate

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you may nominate another person to exercise your data rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity. Contact us at prescopehealth@gmail.com to register a nominee.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with India's Data Protection Board (as established under the DPDP Act) or another competent supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

How to exercise your rights: Email prescopehealth@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Rights Request" and the email address registered to your account. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

1. About Prescope

Prescope is a lifestyle risk-awareness and wellness optimisation tool. It helps users understand lifestyle and genetic factors that may be relevant to their breast health risk profile, and connects them with appropriate screening tests and personalised lifestyle recommendations.

Prescope is not a medical device. It does not diagnose any condition, disease, or illness. It does not provide medical advice. It does not replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. All recommendations generated by the app are reviewed by a licensed practitioner before being presented to you, but they remain lifestyle guidance — not a medical prescription, diagnosis, or treatment plan.

If you have any breast symptoms including pain, discomfort, or a lump, do not use this screening service. Contact your primary care physician immediately for urgent medical assessment.

2. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 years of age or older to use Prescope
  • You must be free of active breast symptoms (pain, discomfort, or a known lump) at the time of entering the screening questionnaire — the app will ask you to confirm this before proceeding
  • You must provide accurate and truthful information — the risk assessment and recommendations are only as reliable as the information you provide
  • You must have the legal capacity to enter into a binding agreement in your jurisdiction

3. The Service

What Prescope does

  • Guides you through a structured risk and lifestyle questionnaire based on an established clinical screening protocol
  • Uses a deterministic rules-based engine to recommend appropriate screening tests based on your answers and, where available, your prior test results
  • Facilitates ordering of those tests through partner laboratories
  • Presents your results with plain-language explanations alongside exact clinical terminology
  • Generates a personalised lifestyle and supplement plan, which is reviewed and approved by a licensed practitioner before you receive it
  • Sends reminders for follow-up tests and a recurring symptom safety check

What Prescope does not do

  • Diagnose any medical condition
  • Provide a medical opinion, medical advice, or a treatment plan
  • Replace a consultation with a qualified doctor or specialist
  • Guarantee any specific health outcome or risk reduction
  • Provide emergency medical care — if you have a medical emergency, call 112 (India) or your local emergency number

4. Consent to genetic and specialised testing

Where you choose to undergo BRCA1/2 genetic testing, circulating tumour cell (CTC) testing, or the SNP risk-modification panel, you will be asked to provide separate, explicit, informed consent for each test type.

These tests sit outside standard national breast-screening guidelines. Their current evidence status, implications of positive and negative results, and what happens after testing will be explained to you in plain language before you are asked to consent.

You may decline any test without losing access to the rest of the Service. Declining does not affect your ability to complete the questionnaire, receive lifestyle recommendations, or order other tests.
